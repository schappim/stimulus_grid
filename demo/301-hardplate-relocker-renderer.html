301 — Hardplate + relocker

Inside-the-door attack-resistance package on a safe. HP = hardplate over the lock (anti-drill); GL = glass relocker (breaks on attack, fires the relocker); TW = thermal/wire-style relocker (fires on heat or boltwork interference). Last pill tells you whether anything has fired.

Safe Hardplate / relocker Brand

A fired relocker is a re-build, not a re-set — the boltwork stays captured until a safe technician disassembles, replaces the relocker assembly, and re-pins. The state pill is a heads-up for the booking.
