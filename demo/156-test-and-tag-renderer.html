156 — Test & Tag (AS/NZS 3760)

Portable electrical equipment register. number = tag ID, class = colour cohort (Red Q1 / Yellow Q2 / Blue Q3 / Green Q4) for spreading test dates across the year. The expiry pill reads "next" instead of "exp" — when the next-due date is past it flips to "expired" red.
