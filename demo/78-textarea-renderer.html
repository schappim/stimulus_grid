78 — Textarea editor

Multi-line text editor sibling to multi-line. Display clamps at N lines; double-click opens a resizable textarea popover. ⌘/Ctrl + Enter commits, Escape cancels.

Customer Notes (3-line clamp) Address (2-line clamp) Changelog (5-line, wide)
The textarea is resizable — drag the bottom-right corner to grow it for long entries.
