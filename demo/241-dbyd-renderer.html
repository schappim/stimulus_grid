241 — DBYD reference

Dial Before You Dig lodgement reference. A DBYD is valid for 28 days from the request date; warn at < 7 days remaining, red beyond. Pair with any excavation, footings, driveway, or directional-bore job so the supervisor doesn't approve a dig on a stale ticket.

Site DBYD ref Lodged Work

Re-lodge before any expired job goes ahead — striking an unmarked gas main is a notifiable safety incident and a guaranteed shutdown of the site. Push Lodged backwards into the past to watch the chip flip warn → expired.
