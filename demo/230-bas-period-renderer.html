230 — BAS period chip

Business activity statement period — fiscal year + quarter. AU's fiscal year runs 1 Jul → 30 Jun, so Q1 is Jul–Sep, Q2 Oct–Dec, Q3 Jan–Mar, Q4 Apr–Jun. The lodgement chip layers lodged, due, overdue, or pending on top.
