302 — Safe boltwork

Active and dead bolts on each edge of a safe door, drawn at scale. A vault-grade safe runs bolts on all four edges; a domestic office safe might only have three live bolts on the hinge side. The trail pill is the total bolt count banded into hi/md/lo.

Safe Boltwork Hinge

The boltwork descriptor uses T/B/L/R for the door edge and A/D for active / dead — e.g. L3A · R3A · T1D · B1D reads "three active bolts left, three active right, one dead pin top and bottom".
