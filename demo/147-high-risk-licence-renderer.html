147 — High-Risk Work Licence (HRWL)

SafeWork-issued High-Risk Work Licence (HRWL). Renewed every 5 years. The class field carries the two-letter competency code: SI / SA / SB scaffolding, WP boom / scissor lifts, DG dogging, RB / RI / RA rigging, CN / C2 / C6 cranes, LF / LO forklifts.
