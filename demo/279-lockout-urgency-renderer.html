279 — Lockout urgency tier

Lockouts arrive over the phone with wildly different stakes. The tier is locksmith-specific — different from a generic job priority cell because it carries dispatch rules: child or pet locked in a car (P0) bumps every other booking; at-night residential (P1) gets after-hours rates; routine spare-key cuts wait their turn.

Caller Urgency Suburb

P0 should also drive a callback path — if the locksmith is more than 20 min away, the dispatcher rings Fire & Rescue. The cell is just the decision boundary, not the SOP.
