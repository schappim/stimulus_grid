236 — Make-safe vs full repair

Most insurance jobs run in two stages — make-safe (board up the broken window, stop the leak, isolate the live circuit) followed by the full repair once the loss-adjuster has scoped and authorised. The chip displays both side-by-side so the coordinator sees the order-of-operations at a glance.

Job Cause Repair phases Adjuster

"Pending → Pending" is the new-claim shape; "Done → Progress" is the healthy mid-claim shape; "Pending → Progress" is the warning sign — a full-repair underway without a make-safe lodged.
