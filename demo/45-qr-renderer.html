45 — QR code renderer

Inline SVG QR code from the cell value. Pure-JS encoder (Reed-Solomon over GF(2⁸), versions 1-10, ECC level Medium) — no library, no bundle bloat, no network call. Hand a phone camera at the cell and you should be able to scan it.

Label QR QR + value Large preview
Capacity: 213 UTF-8 bytes in the largest supported version (v10, ECC=M). Anything longer falls back to a small italic placeholder rather than emitting a broken matrix. Common cell payloads — URLs, invoice IDs, SKUs, Wi-Fi credentials — fit comfortably in v1–v5.
