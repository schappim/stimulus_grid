66 — Plural renderer

Count + plural-sensitive noun via Intl.PluralRules. The zero option lets you provide a separate label for "no items" — often reads better than "0 items".

Project Open PRs Issues Contributors
Try editing a cell to 0 — the renderer swaps to the zero label ("no PRs", "no issues") instead of "0 PRs". Same for switching between 1 and N — the suffix flips cleanly.
