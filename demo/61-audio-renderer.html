61 — Audio renderer (inline native player)

Lightweight sibling of audio-attachment — drops the URL into a native <audio controls> element and lets the browser handle playback, scrub, codec fallback, and accessibility hooks. preload: 'none' by default so a grid of 100 recording URLs doesn't fire 100 range requests on initial render.

Call Recording Duration
When to reach for this vs audio-attachment: audio = a row of small media players in a table cell, no extra chrome. audio-attachment = popover player with Howler.js scrubbing, ±10s skip buttons, and metadata display. Choose based on whether the column is "audio is the data" or "audio is one of many fields".
