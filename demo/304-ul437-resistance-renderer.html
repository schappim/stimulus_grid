304 — UL 437 anti-pick / anti-drill

Independently-tested resistance times against picking and drilling. UL 437 — the recognised high-security cylinder standard — requires at least 10 minutes against each. The vertical tick on each bar is the 10-minute pass line; rows that fall short flag red.

Cylinder Pick / drill resistance Brand

The times are aggregate medians from the test report — not the lowest individual sample. A cylinder that beats the line on pick but loses on drill still fails UL 437 overall.
