20 — Date & time renderers

Four pre-registered renderers built on the platform's Intl.* APIs: date + datetime for locale-aware formatting, relative-time for "3 days ago" / "in 2 hours" with the absolute timestamp in a hover tooltip, and duration for "2h 14m" / "00:23:45" out of seconds or milliseconds. All four accept a Date, an ISO string, or a millisecond epoch — the same shapes the grid's existing type="date" sort/filter paths handle.

Custom variants: the four renderers above are the pre-registered defaults (US locale, medium date style, compact duration). Register your own at any time — registerRenderer('date-au', renderers.date({ locale: 'en-AU', dateStyle: 'full' })), registerRenderer('duration-clock', renderers.duration({ unit: 'sec', style: 'clock' })), etc. The Stopwatch column above is one such variant.
