240 — NMI (National Meter Identifier)

The 10- or 11-character electricity meter ID. Sparkies, retailers and DNSPs all key off this number. The first two digits identify the jurisdiction (NSW 41xx, VIC 61xx, QLD 31xx…) so we derive a state badge from the prefix. Last char (if 11 chars) is the AEMO check digit — not validated here.
