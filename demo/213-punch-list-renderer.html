213 — Punch-list summary

Compact "N open of M total" with a severity tint (worst-open wins) and a thin progress bar of closed items. The dispatcher gets a glanceable handover-readiness across a portfolio of projects.

Project Open Total Punch list

The renderer reads three pre-computed fields — ratio, worst, worstLabel — derived once from the underlying list of items. Editing any of open / total / worst re-derives them so the bar stays in sync.
