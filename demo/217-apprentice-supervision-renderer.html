217 — Apprentice year + supervision ratio

Year of apprenticeship plus the on-site supervision ratio (e.g. 1:1, 1:2, 1:3). For unlicensed or restricted work, the legal ratio dictates whether a job can run at all — the cell flags a ratio that exceeds the apprentice-year limit. Qualified workers render with a neutral chip.

Worker Trade Year / Ratio Supervisor

The legal ratio is computed in the data layer (e.g. Y1 → 1:1, Y2 → 1:2, Y3+ → 1:3). The renderer just paints what it's told — a saved view like "Ratio breach" filters on check = 'bad'.
