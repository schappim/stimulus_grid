220 — Clock-in geo-stamp

When a technician taps Start job, the app captures the time plus the device's GPS. The cell prints the timestamp and the distance to the booked site — green if within 100 m, amber up to 500 m, red beyond. The pin button opens Google Maps so a supervisor can verify against street view.
