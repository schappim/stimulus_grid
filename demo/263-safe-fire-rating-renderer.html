263 — Safe fire rating

Minutes-to-failure at standard fire test temperature, plus the interior media spec — paper (≤ 177 °C internal), media (≤ 65 °C), data (≤ 52 °C and humidity-controlled). A "1-hour safe" rated for paper won't protect a hard drive.

Safe Fire rating Contents

Mismatched rows (paper-rated safe with media contents, or media-rated safe used for data) are exactly the conversation a locksmith wants to have before the next site survey, not after the fire.
