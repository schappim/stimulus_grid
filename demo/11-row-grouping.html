11 — Row grouping & aggregation

Group rows by one or more columns and roll up aggregates per group. Grouping is declared with data-grid-row-group-cols-value and data-grid-agg-funcs-value, or driven at runtime via gridApi.setRowGroupColumns(). Click a group row to expand/collapse. Sort, filter and virtual scrolling all still apply.
