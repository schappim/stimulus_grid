243 — Storeys / level / unit

Building-geometry chip: total storeys, the work-level, and the unit. Plus a heights flag that triggers WaHO sign-off above 2 m and a "needs lift / swing-stage" flag above L4.

Site Storeys / Level / Unit Level Unit

Storey total is intentionally distinct from work-level — a 12-storey tower can still have a ground-floor job. The heights pill triggers on the work-level, not the building height.
