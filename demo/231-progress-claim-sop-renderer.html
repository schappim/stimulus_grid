231 — Subbie progress claim (Security of Payment Act)

Sub-contractor claim under the SoP Act. The clock is statutory — the head-contractor has a fixed number of business days to issue a payment schedule, and missing it forfeits the right to dispute. The chip flags ok, warn (≤ 2 BD left), breach (schedule overdue), scheduled, paid.
