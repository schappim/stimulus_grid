76 — Date range renderer

Display + editor for a start/end date pair. Compact formatting collapses repeated month / year ("Jun 4 – 18, 2026" vs "Jun 4 – Jul 2, 2026" vs "Dec 4, 2025 – Jan 3, 2026"). Double-click opens a two-month calendar; click start, click end. Stores { start, end } ISO dates.

Trip Dates Booking window
Click a date in the left calendar to set the start; click a later date (either calendar) to set the end. The fill bar highlights every day in between.
