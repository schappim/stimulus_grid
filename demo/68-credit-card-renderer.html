68 — Credit card renderer

Luhn-validated, brand-detected card number display. Defaults to PCI-friendly masked form (last 4 visible). Detects Visa / Mastercard / Amex / Discover / JCB / Diners by IIN prefix and length.

Customer Card Exp Last charge
Why default to masked? PCI-DSS § 3.3 — Primary Account Numbers must be masked when displayed; only the first 6 and last 4 digits are reproducible without a clear business need. The default here only shows the last 4. Pass mask: false only when the column is a one-shot reveal flow.
