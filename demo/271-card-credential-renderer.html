Access card row: facility/card number with the format chip that tells you whether it's a 1980s 26-bit prox you can clone with a $20 Proxmark, a MIFARE Classic that's broken-but-still-everywhere, or a DESFire EV3 / iCLASS Seos that's actually doing crypto.
