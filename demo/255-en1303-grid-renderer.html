255 — EN 1303 grid

European cylinder standard. Six digits — category, durability, door mass, fire/smoke, corrosion+temp, key security, attack resistance — rendered as a tile strip. Key-security (pos 6) and attack-resistance (pos 7) drive the colour, since they're what an insurer looks at.

Lock EN 1303 Brand

Pos 7 (attack) is rare on Australian cylinders — you'll mostly see EN-stamped product in commercial fit-outs spec'd to European code. A 0 in that position means the cylinder wasn't tested for drill/pick — not that it failed.
