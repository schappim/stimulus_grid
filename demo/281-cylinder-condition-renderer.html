281 — Cylinder condition on arrival

The physical state of the lock at the moment the locksmith arrives. Drives whether the job line reads "open + rekey" or "drill out + replace + repair strike plate" — and gives the customer something concrete on the invoice when they ring back asking why the bill wasn't $99.
