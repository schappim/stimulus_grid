268 — Remote / fob frequency

The RF band and modulation a fob talks on — drives which tester or sniffer you reach for, and whether you can clone the remote at all. Fixed-code remotes (red) can be cloned with a basic copier; rolling or bidirectional remotes need the OEM dealer tool or the matching aftermarket programmer.

Vehicle / device RF + code Buttons

The band determines local regulations too — 433.92 MHz is the European/AU norm, 315 MHz is North-American, 868 MHz is short-range (alarms, sensors). Match the band to the country the vehicle was delivered in, not the country it's parked in.
