226 — Supplier price-rank

Each part exists at multiple suppliers — Reece, Tradelink, Plumbing Plus, Beacon, Bunnings — and the rank chip flags the cheapest, the preferred (loyalty / trade-account / service), the next-cheapest, and the over-priced. Preferred isn't always cheapest, which is exactly the point — the buyer sees both.

SKU Part Supplier Rank Price ex

The rank is computed per-SKU group: cheapest gets cheap; the row with preferred: true gets preferred regardless of price; the next-up gets next; everything else past 1.15× cheapest reads expensive. Edit a price to re-rank the group.
