247 — BAL rating

Bushfire Attack Level under AS 3959. LOW means no specific BAL construction; BAL-12.5–40 step up the radiant-heat exposure (kW/m²); BAL-FZ (Flame Zone) means direct flame contact and is effectively unbuildable in many councils.
