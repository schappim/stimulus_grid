256 — Group-by-date sticky day headers

Built on the grid's groupBy, the day header sticks to the top of the viewport as you scroll and carries a running-totals strip — jobs, hours, $ value — per day. The today / tomorrow / this-week / later rotation maps a tradie's mental model better than a calendar grid.

Job # When Customer Tech Hrs $ ex

Sticky group rows are achieved via position: sticky; top: 0 on the group td. Per-day totals are rendered into the group label after the grid emits grid:rowDataChanged.
