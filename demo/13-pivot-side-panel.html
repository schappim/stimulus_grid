13 — Pivot mode & side panel

The side panel drives row groups, pivot columns and value aggregations through drag-and-drop. Toggle Pivot mode at the top to reshape the data: rows by country, columns by sport, cells aggregating gold medals. Click an SUM badge on a value chip to cycle through sum → avg → count → min → max.
