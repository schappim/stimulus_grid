204 — SLA countdown / breach

Contractual response-time clock — "respond within 4 business hours" or "make-safe within 2h on a P1". The pill updates live as you re-schedule a job: double-click the Due by column to edit the deadline and watch the pill recompute (ok / warn / breach). States met, missed, and paused are terminal and don't get recomputed by the deadline change.

Job # Customer Contract SLA Due by

Double-click any Due by cell — the picker is a native <input type="datetime-local">. On commit, the demo's grid:cellValueChanged handler recomputes the pill via applyTransaction. Try moving an "ok" job into the past to flip it to breach, or push a "breach" deadline into the future to recover it.
