204 — SLA countdown / breach

Contractual response-time clock — "respond within 4 business hours" or "make-safe within 2h on a P1". Drives whether the job earns or loses money. The pill states are ok (plenty of time), warn (≤ 30 min buffer), breach (overdue), met (closed inside the window), missed (closed late), and paused (clock stopped while waiting on the customer).

Pair the SLA pill with setRowClassRules so the entire row pulses red when in breach. The pill itself can sort by the underlying minutes-remaining so the dispatcher's "most-at-risk" view is one click.
