253 — Cylinder size

Cylinder geometry that drives whether a replacement actually fits. Euro profiles show outside / inside lengths split around the screw notch; mortice locks show case-size + backset. Asymmetric euro (outside > inside + 10) gets a snap-attack warning chip.

Lock Geometry Type

Euro sizes are outside/inside in mm (always outside first when standing outside the door). Mortice shows case × backset.
