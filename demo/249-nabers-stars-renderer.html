249 — NABERS / energy stars

NABERS rating (0–6 stars) for commercial buildings. Sub-4★ triggers Commercial Building Disclosure flags for offices > 1,000 m². The chip combines the star row, the numeric rating, and a banded background so the worst rows stand out.

Building Stars NABERS NLA (m²)

Star fractions (e.g. 4.5★) render half-filled in production; for simplicity this demo rounds to the nearest whole star.
