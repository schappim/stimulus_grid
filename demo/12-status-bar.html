12 — Status bar

Enabled with data-grid-status-bar-value="true". The footer shows the row count (with filtered/total split when filtered) and the selection count, and — whenever you select a cell range — sum / avg / count / min / max of the numeric values in that range, like a spreadsheet.

Athlete Country Sport Age Gold Silver Bronze

Try it: drag across a few Gold/Silver/Bronze cells, or hold ⌘/Ctrl and drag a second range — the right side of the status bar updates live. Filter the Country column to see "Rows: X of Y" on the left.
