250 — Keyway / profile

The blank's keyway determines which cylinder a cut will physically enter. The little silhouette is the cross-section a cutter would see from the bow end — useful when reaching for a tin of SC1 blanks and not grabbing SC4 by mistake.

Site / lock Keyway Class Blanks

Silhouettes are stylised — they're not pin-accurate to the real keyway, but distinct enough that a tech reaching for SC1 vs C4 won't confuse them on a busy bench.
