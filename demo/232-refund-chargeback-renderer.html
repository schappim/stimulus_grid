232 — Refund / chargeback indicator

Distinguishes a regular refund (partial / full) from a card-scheme chargeback (chargeback / disputed while the bank investigation runs). Disputed rows pulse — they cost time, fees, and brand-trust if the merchant loses.

Invoice # Customer Original Refunded Refund / CB Reason

The kind field is the system of record — the percent is computed from refunded ÷ original so editing either input column re-derives the chip.
