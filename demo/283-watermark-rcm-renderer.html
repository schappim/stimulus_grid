283 — WaterMark / RCM compliance

WaterMark (plumbing products under WMTS) and RCM (Regulatory Compliance Mark for electrical, electronic and telecom) are the two product-compliance marks every Australian tradie has to eyeball before installing. Two independent badges per product so a sparkie / plumber sees applicability and validity at a glance.

SKU Product Compliance Cert #

A "no" badge on a mandatory mark (WaterMark on a plumbing product; RCM on a 240V product) is grounds to refuse the install — the contractor wears liability.
