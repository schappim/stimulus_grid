16 — Master/detail rows

Click the chevron next to any order to expand a detail panel beneath the row, showing the order's status, customer + total in a header strip and a nested grid of its line items. Enabled with data-grid-master-detail-value="true", a data-grid-detail-template-value pointing at a <template>, and data-grid-detail-rows-key-value="lineItems" to feed the inner grid from each master row.
