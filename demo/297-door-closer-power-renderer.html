297 — Door closer power (EN 1-7)

EN 1154 door-closer power size — 1 (tiny internal cupboards) through 7 (large external double doors). AS 1428.1 / DDA caps the closing force on accessible doors, so anything above size 3 on a path-of- travel door is non-compliant.

Door EN Closer power Accessible path

Adjustable closers cover a range (e.g. an EN 2–5 closer can be spring-tensioned to fit a size-3 door). The cell shows the set size, not the closer's adjustment range.
