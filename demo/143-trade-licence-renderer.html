143 — Trade licence (AU contractor licence)

Australian contractor licence card. Each state has its own scheme — NSW Fair Trading, QBCC, VBA, SA CBS, Service Tasmania — and a different number format. This renderer accepts the common-denominator object shape and surfaces a state badge, monospaced licence number, optional trade class, and a traffic-light expiry pill (green / yellow / orange / red).

Tradie Trade Contractor licence
Value shape: { number, state, class, expires }. Strings (just the licence number) also render. The expiry pill goes green > 90 days out, yellow inside 90, orange inside 30, red once past — and the title attribute shows exact days remaining.
