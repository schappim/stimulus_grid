227 — Special-order ETA

For parts not held in branch stock. The chip shows the order state, the courier-tracked ETA, and the supplier — and flips to delayed the moment the ETA slips past the original promise. Edit the ETA cell to push or recover a date.

PO # Part Special order ETA Promised

Promised ETA is captured once at PO send and never changes. The delay flag flips on whenever eta > promisedEta, so a saved view "PO running late" picks them up without thresholds.
