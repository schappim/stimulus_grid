206 — Re-attendance counter

Counts how many physical visits a single job has burned. Visit 1 of 1 is normal; 2 of 2 is worth a sit-down; 3+ auto-flags the row so a supervisor reviews root cause — wrong diagnosis, missed parts, customer not on site? Each repeat visit destroys the margin.
