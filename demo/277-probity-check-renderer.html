277 — Probity / police-check

Annual probity status for technicians who touch restricted-key systems or SCEC-listed product. Different from a generic licence or insurance certificate — this is renewed every 12 months and directly gates which jobs the dispatcher can hand them.

Technician Probity Last sensitive job

A blocked tech doesn't mean a bad tech — it means the dispatcher needs to route restricted work elsewhere until the new check clears. Most failures here are paperwork (expired ID, address change), not anything found in the search itself.
