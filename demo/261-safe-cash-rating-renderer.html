261 — Safe cash rating

Two figures that determine what an insurer will cover sitting in the safe — overnight (out of business hours) and working hours (typically 10× the overnight figure). The chip flips red when the on-site cash exceeds the overnight rating: that's a claim that won't pay.

Site Cash rating On-site $

Edit the On-site $ column to push the rating into the red. Real insurer policies sometimes also have a transit rider — that's a separate cell, not modelled here.
