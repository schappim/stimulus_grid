296 — Maglock holding force

Electromagnetic lock spec at-a-glance — pounds force plus the kg equivalent. The fit pill flips red when the maglock is undersized for the door (a 600 lb single-leaf is fine on a 35 kg internal door, but not on a 90 kg glass entrance).

Door Force (lbs) Leaf (kg) Maglock

Rule-of-thumb: pick a maglock whose lb rating is at least 10× the door-leaf weight in kg. The fit pill applies that rule; edit the lbs column to see it flip.
