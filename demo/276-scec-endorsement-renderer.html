276 — SCEC endorsement

Australian Government Security Construction & Equipment Committee endorsement. Three things get endorsed: products (e.g. SCEC Class C container), facilities (Zone 1–5 rooms / strongrooms), and endorsed locksmiths — only an endorsed locksmith can touch SCEC-listed product in a Zone 4/5 environment.

Subject SCEC Agency

A facility's zone is the headline number on a job sheet — Zone 4 means TOP SECRET, Zone 5 is for the very small set of compartmented spaces. Mismatching a SCEC product with a facility zone is a notifiable security event.
