274 — Lockout / tagout state

A door (or strike, or controller relay) that's been deliberately taken out of service while work is happening. The cell shows the state, the technician who locked it out, and how long it's been pulled — anything sitting in LOTO for more than a shift is a handover risk.

Door / asset State Reason

A LOTO isn't a status to set on the access list — it's a physical padlock and a tag. The cell mirrors the physical reality back into the schedule so the front desk doesn't keep trying to badge through a tagged-out door.
