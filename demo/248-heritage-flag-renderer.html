248 — Heritage / conservation flag

Heritage overlays restrict work scope — façade material, roof type, fenestration, signage, even paint colours. Five levels: none, contributory (within a conservation area), local, state, national. The level dictates which authority has to give development consent.

Site Heritage Authority Restriction

State- and national-listed items can't be touched without delegated consent. Even paint colour on a contributory row is regulated in some LGAs (City of Sydney, Glebe, Surry Hills).
