266 — Transponder chip

Identifies what's inside the head of an automotive key. The transponder name tells you which programmer to reach for; the crypto chip is the difference between "ten minutes on the bench" and "we need to pull the immobiliser unit".

Vehicle Transponder Programmer

Fixed-code chips (Philips 33, Megamos 13) are auto-locksmith bread-and-butter — anything with rolling AES (DST80, HITAG AES, Megamos AES) needs current tooling and a paid subscription.
