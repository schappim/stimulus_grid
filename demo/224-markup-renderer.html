224 — Markup %

Cost in, charge out, markup percent computed and tinted by margin band. Loss (< 0%) is red, thin (0–15%) is amber, healthy (15–60%) green, fat (> 60%) blue — for the buyer to spot pricing anomalies fast. Edit either price column to see the bar re-derive.

SKU Part Cost ex Charge ex Markup

Markup % uses (charge - cost) / cost × 100. If you prefer margin % ((charge - cost) / charge × 100), keep the underlying numbers identical and just rename the cell.
