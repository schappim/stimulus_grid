144 — White Card (Construction Induction Card)

The General Construction Induction Card (CIC, "White Card") — required on every Australian construction site. Issued by RTOs nationally so the state on the card is the holder's state of issue. Double-click to open the four-field editor (state / number / class / expiry).

Worker Trade White Card
CICs do not federally carry an expiry, but most builders self-impose a 2-year refresh window. Pass expires in the value object and the traffic-light pill kicks in.
