Mains amperage and phase configuration. Lo (≤ 40 A single-phase) sites can't take an EV charger or a 3.5 kW reverse-cycle without an upgrade; 3-phase sites can drive heavier loads but the runs cost more. Edit either column to re-derive the sizing band.
