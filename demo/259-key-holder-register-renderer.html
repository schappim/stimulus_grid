259 — Key holder register

Whole-system view of who has what. Three numbers and a tiny stacked bar: how many keys are in the field, how many have been returned to the safe, how many have been declared lost. A lost-bar that grows even slightly is the trigger for a re-key conversation.

System Register Last audit

The numbers refer to keys, not key-holders — one tenancy in a strata might hold three CKs. Click-through from the cell (not implemented in this demo) would normally drill to the per-key holder list.
