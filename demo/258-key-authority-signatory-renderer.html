258 — Key authority signatory

Restricted-key authority lives or dies on one signature. The cell answers "are we allowed to cut this?" — name of the signatory, has the wet-ink form been scanned in, and is the authority still inside its renewal window. A red expiry means the cut goes back in the queue until the customer re-signs.

Restricted system Authority signatory Role

A signatory who has left the org is not the same as an expired authority — keep both flags (we model "on file" + "expiry") so a new request can be routed to whoever the company has nominated next.
