205 — Quote status (sent / viewed / accepted / …)

The funnel before an invoice exists. Viewed is the high-value signal — most quoting tools track e-mail open / PDF open and the follow-up window is < 48h before the lead cools. Edit the Expires column to push or pull a quote's deadline; if it lands before "now" the status auto-flips to expired, and bumping it forward restores the previous status.

Quote # Customer Total Status Sent Expires

Both Sent and Expires are editable datetime cells. The handler watches grid:cellValueChanged on expires and flips the status to expired when the deadline passes (preserving the prior status under __prevStatus so a forward-shift can restore it). Accepted and declined are terminal and never get overridden.
