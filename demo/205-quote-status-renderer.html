205 — Quote status (sent / viewed / accepted / …)

The funnel before an invoice exists. Viewed is the high-value signal — most quoting tools track e-mail open / PDF open and the follow-up window is < 48h before the lead cools. Pair this with the SLA pill (#204) to chase quotes that haven't been touched.
