234 — Authority-limit pill (insurance work)

Insurance-authorised jobs have a $ cap above which the contractor must seek a fresh authority before continuing. The pill reads "$1,500 → $1,320" with state under / at (within 5%) / over, so a coordinator spots the rows that need a re-authorisation call before any work goes ahead.

The "at" band (within 5% of the cap) is a deliberate buffer — once you're 90%+ of the way through the authority and still finding more work, it's cheaper to call ahead than to argue post-hoc.
