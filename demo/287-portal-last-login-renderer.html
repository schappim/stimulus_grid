254 — Customer portal last login

Engagement recency pill: fresh (today), active (< 7d), aged (< 30d), stale (≥ 30d), never. For a self-service portal it's a great win-back-campaign signal; for an FSM portal it's a "customer doesn't know where to find the invoice" tell.
