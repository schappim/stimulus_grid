265 — Time-lock window

ATM and CIT (cash-in-transit) safes don't open on demand — they have a fixed daily window, plus a time delay after the right combination is entered. The dial sits across a 24-hour bar; the red tick is now (rendered against demo time 09:30).

Safe Time-lock window Delay

The amber band is the time-delay after the combination is entered; the green band is the daily open window. If the red tick sits in grey, the safe physically can't open until the next window.
