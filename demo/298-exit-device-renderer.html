298 — Panic / emergency exit device

Push-bar / touch-bar / lever hardware on egress doors with the certification stamp. EN 1125 is the panic standard for places where the occupants are untrained (cinemas, shopping centres); EN 179 is for places they're not (offices, plant rooms). Public-occupancy rows without EN 1125 flag red.

Door Exit device Occupancy

The AS 1428.1 chip is independent of EN 179/1125 — it covers the one-handed-operation and force-to-operate parts of the standard. A lever-trim retrofit can deliver AS 1428.1 over an EN 179 base.
