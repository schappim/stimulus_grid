257 — Restricted system + patent expiry

Restricted keyways trade on the manufacturer's patent — once it expires anyone can cut a copy. The chip shows the system; the countdown shows how long until the system stops being commercially restricted. Drives whether you can quote a 10-year MKS without a keyway-migration line item.
