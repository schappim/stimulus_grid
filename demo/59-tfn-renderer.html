59 — TFN renderer (Tax File Number — always masked)

Tax File Numbers are legally never displayed in full (ATO + Privacy Act). This renderer always shows them as ••• ••• XYZ — last 3 digits visible, no exceptions, no opt-out. Use it alongside mask for the rest of your sensitive PII.

Employee TFN DOB Phone (masked)
Why no showFull option? Regulatory requirement, not a styling choice — a "show full TFN" flag would be a footgun waiting to be misconfigured. If you genuinely need a cleartext TFN (employer payroll system, etc.), do it through a one-shot reveal flow, not a column-wide renderer setting.
