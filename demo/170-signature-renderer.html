170 — Signature (customer sign-off + pad editor)

Inline signature thumbnail — 80×32 by default, click to open full image. Double-click the cell to open the signature pad (mouse / touch / stylus all draw). Save commits via grid:cellValueChanged; the image is auto-trimmed to the inked bounds and stamped with signedAt (today's date).

Cmd/Ctrl+Enter saves from inside the pad. Esc cancels. Empty pad saves null.
