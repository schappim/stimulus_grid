278 — Insurance-approved badge

Independent attack-resistance grades that map directly onto an insurance cover line. Sold Secure (Bronze → Diamond), CEN attack grade (3–5), Secured by Design — the badge a retailer points at when the customer asks "is it the lock that's covered?".

Product Approval Use-case

These badges are independent of the AS 4145 or EN 1303 stamping — Sold Secure tests the assembled product (lock + door + frame) the way an attacker actually approaches it, not just the cylinder.
