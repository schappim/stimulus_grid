14 — Column header groups & pinned bottom row

Stacked column headers — declare nested header groups with data-grid-column-groups-value — paired with a sticky bottom row that holds grand totals over every filtered leaf row. Both features are independent and compose with sort, filter, pagination, and virtual scrolling.
