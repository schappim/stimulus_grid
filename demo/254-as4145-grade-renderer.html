254 — AS 4145 dual grade

Australian Standard 4145 cylinder grading: a security level (SL1–SL7) and a durability level (S1–S8). Insurers usually require SL ≥ 5 and S ≥ 5 on commercial premises — rows that don't clear that bar show a red insurance chip.
