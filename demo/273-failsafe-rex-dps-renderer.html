273 — Fail-safe / fail-secure + REX/DPS

Power-loss behaviour and the door-loop sensors in a single cell. Fail-safe unlocks on power loss (egress, fire-rated paths); fail-secure stays locked (asset protection, safe rooms). REX/DPS pills light up when those signals are wired back to the controller.

Door Lock + sensors Path

A REX without a DPS is a "magic exit" — the controller will mute the alarm for the REX hold time and then let the door swing open unobserved. Use both for any door that's not free-egress on the inside handle.
