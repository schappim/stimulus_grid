285 — Call log entry

Direction (inbound / outbound / missed / voicemail), duration in mm:ss, and a recording-attached badge. Voicemail is its own direction because it's neither connection nor miss — it's a one-way utterance you have to listen to.

When Call Note

Recordings ought to carry a retention policy — most CRMs keep call audio for 30 / 90 / 180 days then archive. The badge here is a stub you'd hook to a presigned URL.
