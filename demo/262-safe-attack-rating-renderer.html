262 — Safe attack rating

EN 1143-1 (Grade 0–VI) or UL TL/TRTL ratings — what a safe is certified to resist and for how long. The chip carries the standard + grade; the trailing pill is the indicative resistance time in minutes (RTI) for partial / full access.

Safe Attack rating Location

EN gives a single combined grade; UL splits TL (tool) from TR (torch) from TX (explosive). When mapping to insurance, only the lower of the two ratings counts.
